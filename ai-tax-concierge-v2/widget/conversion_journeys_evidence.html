Journey B: MTD eligibility → suitable plan

Customer
My qualifying income was £55,000 in 2024-25. Do I need MTD from April 2026?
SIMBA
Yes — if your qualifying income was more than £50,000 for 2024-25, you need to use Making Tax Digital for Income Tax from 6 April 2026. Qualifying income means self-employment and property income before expenses (turnover/gross), not profit. Official guidance: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/find-out-if-and-when-you-need-to-use-making-tax-digital-for-income-tax
