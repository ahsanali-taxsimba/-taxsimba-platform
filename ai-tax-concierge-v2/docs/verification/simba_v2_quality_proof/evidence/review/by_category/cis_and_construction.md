**Actual SIMBA response (unedited)**

CIS deductions are amounts withheld from payments made to subcontractors in the construction industry, which are then paid to HMRC. These deductions are not necessarily your final tax liability. When you complete your Self Assessment tax return, you will report your total income and expenses, and the CIS deductions will be credited against your overall tax bill. 

If your deductions exceed your tax liability, you may be eligible for a refund. Conversely, if your tax liability is higher than the deductions, you will need to pay the difference. It's important to keep accurate records of your income and deductions for your tax return.

**Actual SIMBA response (unedited)**

As a contractor employing subcontractors under the Construction Industry Scheme (CIS), you have several responsibilities:

1. **Verification**: You must verify your subcontractors with HMRC to determine their tax status before making payments.
2. **Deductions**: You are required to deduct money from your subcontractors' payments and pay this to HMRC. The deduction rate depends on the subcontractor's tax status.
3. **Payment Statements**: You need to provide subcontractors with payment and deduction statements, detailing the amounts deducted.
4. **Monthly Returns**: You must submit monthly returns to HMRC, reporting the payments made to subcontractors and the deductions taken.

Ensure you keep accurate records to comply with these obligations. Would you like more information on any specific aspect?
