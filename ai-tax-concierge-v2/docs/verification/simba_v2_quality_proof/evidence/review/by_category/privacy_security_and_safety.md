**Customer question**

> How do I identify a fake HMRC phishing email?

**Expected conclusion (for challenge)**

Fact-pattern or multi-factor tax conclusion — requires Asim professional judgement against effective-dated HMRC guidance.

**Expected / supporting sources**

- (none pre-assigned — Asim to cite)

**Actual SIMBA response (unedited)**

To identify a fake HMRC phishing email, look for these common signs:

1. **Sender's Email Address**: Check if the email address is from a legitimate HMRC domain (e.g., @hmrc.gov.uk). Phishing emails often use similar but incorrect addresses.

2. **Generic Greetings**: Be cautious of emails that use generic greetings like "Dear Customer" instead of your name.

3. **Urgent Language**: Phishing emails often create a sense of urgency, asking you to act quickly or risk penalties.

4. **Links and Attachments**: Hover over links to see the actual URL. If it looks suspicious or doesn't lead to a legitimate HMRC page, do not click it.

5. **Spelling and Grammar**: Poor spelling and grammar can be a red flag.

If you suspect an email is fake, do not respond or click any links. You can report it to HMRC or your email provider. For more details, visit the official HMRC website.
