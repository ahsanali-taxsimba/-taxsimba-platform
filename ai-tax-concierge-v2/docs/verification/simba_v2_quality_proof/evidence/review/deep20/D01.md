### Turn 4 — Customer

What records should I keep?

### Turn 4 — SIMBA

As a self-employed freelancer, you should keep records of your business income and expenses. This includes:

1. **Income**: Invoices, receipts, and bank statements showing all income received.
2. **Expenses**: Receipts and invoices for business-related costs, such as materials, travel, and utilities.
3. **Bank Statements**: Records of all transactions related to your business.

It's essential to keep these records for at least five years after the 31 January submission deadline of the relevant tax year. Digital record-keeping is particularly important under Making Tax Digital (MTD). 

For more details, you can refer to the official guidance: [HMRC Self-Employed Records](https://www.gov.uk/self-employed-records). If you have further questions about specific records, feel free to ask!
