What documents are usually needed for Self Assessment?

### Turn 3 — SIMBA

For Self Assessment, you typically need the following documents:

1. **Income Records**: Details of all income sources, including self-employment earnings, rental income, and any other untaxed income.
2. **Bank Statements**: To verify income and expenses.
3. **Expense Receipts**: Records of business expenses, including invoices and receipts.
4. **P60/P45**: If you have employment income, these documents show your earnings and tax deducted.
5. **National Insurance Number**: For identification purposes.

It's always a good idea to check the official guidance on GOV.UK for any specific requirements related to your situation.
