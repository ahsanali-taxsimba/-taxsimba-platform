|---|---|---|---|

| Trading / property allowance | https://www.gov.uk/guidance/tax-free-allowances-on-property-and-trading-income | Up to **£1,000** relief — not a free pass to ignore SA when trading income already **> £1,000** | Current allowance |

| Dividends | https://www.gov.uk/tax-on-dividends | Dividend allowance / rates; SA depends on total income | Year-specific allowances |
