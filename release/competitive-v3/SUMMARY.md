# TaxSimba Competitive Revision 3 — completed work and remaining gaps

The latest saved V2 source was continued, not rebuilt. The first 20 remain first, followed by the remaining 100. All 120 stable URLs and their artwork/download routes are retained.

## Delivered

- First 20: expanded practical guidance, including fee comparisons, handover requests, exact correction stages, missing-UTR escalation and a property deadline example.
- Remaining 100: replaced the existing example in every article with a distinct preparation exercise and a decision to resolve from evidence.
- All 120 worksheets: added matching practical tasks, evidence-gap prompts and follow-up responsibility.
- Three interactive arithmetic tools across five articles: full quote cost, corrected cumulative totals and 60-day calendar counting.
- Source-backed specialist additions: CIS statement retrieval; 2025/26 close-company director details; loss-relief routes; transition-profit spreading. Clarified the new-source quick answer without duplicating the already-present body explanation.

## Verification

Production build and article/metadata/order/asset regression checks passed. Calculator tests include VAT, invalid values, corrections, invalid dates and leap-year arithmetic. All 120 article HTTP pages, 120 downloads, 360 image responses and 120 sitemap entries passed. Five tool panels and their jump links were present in server-rendered HTML. No live financial transaction or customer message was sent.

A new browser/mobile pass could not be completed because Chromium failed to download. Input interactions, rendered layouts and accessibility therefore need checking on staging. Live signup, auth, payments, email and backend delivery remain untested in this pass. The build uses existing lint/type-check skips.

## What the competitive findings now mean

The revision addresses concrete usability gaps; it does not establish that every article beats every relevant competitor. Broader preparation examples are not a substitute for detailed specialist review. Real product screenshots and filing evidence, qualified reviewer attribution, reader testing and final competitive reassessment remain open. None is marked independently approved or best in the market.

Ready for Cursor integration and Toxel staging. Not cleared for immediate production publication.
