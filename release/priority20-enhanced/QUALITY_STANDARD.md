# Priority twenty — usefulness and maintenance standard

## What changed

- Twenty distinct scenarios add practical reasoning instead of simply restating introductory definitions.
- Three-row tables distinguish inputs, evidence and actions for each specific problem.
- Forty follow-up answers address boundaries: missing records, mismatched filing evidence, extra service work, late registration and other relevant situations.
- Twenty offline preparation worksheets give readers a reusable next step without a signup form. They contain blank prompts and no personal data.
- Full article text and existing SEO fields are retained. More words are not treated as an automatic ranking benefit.
- Examples are explicitly illustrative. They are not invented customer case studies, live quotes, estimates of a reader's tax bill or success claims.

## Before claiming a market lead

Record actual expert review and credentials when it happens; do not fabricate a named reviewer. After launch, assess search impressions, relevant queries, click-through and enquiries alongside mobile performance and accessibility. Use those results to prioritise revisions rather than promise a ranking outcome.

Recheck affected guides when HMRC changes a deadline, reporting process, threshold, agent permission or transition rule. Recheck service wording when the actual TaxSimba package changes. Preserve original publication dates; change the content-update date when a substantive edit is made. Update or withdraw any example that no longer fits the applicable rule. This document does not create an automatic monitoring task.
