# Illustration prompt specifications

Built-in image-generation tool used, one call for each of ten distinct article banners. Style reference: the supplied IMG_9695.png editorial artwork. Each prompt requested a wide 3:1 finished banner, edge-to-edge, warm cream/forest green/sage, fine ink and painterly storybook texture, personable adults, subtle humour and British home-office daylight. No phone screenshot, UI, black bars, logo, headlines or important wording inside the image; webpage content remains HTML.

Scenes in launch order:
1. Client uses a magnifying glass to examine a blank price tag while an accountant explains a portfolio representing bookkeeping, updates and an annual return.
2. Craftsperson and accountant compare varied income documents and a price tag, with workshop and rental-property vignettes.
3. Client joining a trail of reporting calendars; accountant organises earlier receipts before continuing to the next period.
4. Two accountants hand over a closed green records folder, overseen by their client.
5. Reporting-calendar/records tray separate from a tax-budget savings jar and coins.
6. Completed-year folder kept separate from ongoing digital records, with accountant and client.
7. Growing steps of records connected by a ribbon, illustrating cumulative year-to-date information.
8. Client identifies a receipt in the wrong pile and accountant corrects its filing, with an audit trail rather than destroyed records.
9. Freelancer checks the letterbox while preparing organised tax records at a home-office desk.
10. Employment bag and side-business materials accompany two streams of records combined into one personal-return folder.

Rendered outputs were inspected and the final alt descriptions in ARTICLE_MANIFEST.json describe the relevant meaning of each scene. Responsive WebP conversion creates 480×160, 960×320 and 1440×480 variants. Cards use empty alt because their adjacent linked title supplies the accessible name; full article heroes use descriptive alt text. Mobile article heroes crop centrally; headings and answer text do not depend on that crop.
