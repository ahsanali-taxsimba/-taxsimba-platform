  1. Open the official GOV.UK guidance linked below and confirm the rule that applies to your tax year and income type.
  2. Write down the tax year dates you are dealing with (6 April to 5 April) so seasonal wording does not get mixed with an earlier year.
  3. Gather the records that support the figures you will report: invoices, platform statements, agent statements, expense evidence and previous submissions.
