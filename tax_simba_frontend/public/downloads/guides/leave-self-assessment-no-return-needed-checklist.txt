Preparation worksheet

Optional offline planning aid, not a tax return or filing confirmation.
Keep completed notes securely; never include passwords or one-time codes.

WORK THROUGH THIS EXAMPLE
If the income source has stopped, list remaining income, gains and any notices before asking HMRC to remove the filing requirement. Retain the response for the specific year. Stopping an accountancy subscription is not confirmation that HMRC withdrew the return.

DECISION TO RESOLVE
Which year has HMRC confirmed, and what older obligations remain?
