WORK THROUGH THIS EXAMPLE
A payslip and a private pension statement describe contributions differently. Label which amounts are relief at source, net pay or salary sacrifice before supplying a total. Adding every displayed figure can deduct the same contribution twice.

DECISION TO RESOLVE
What method applies to each payment, and which figure belongs in the tax calculation?
