TaxSimba — CIS Payment and Deduction Statements: What Subcontractors Need to Keep
Preparation worksheet

Optional offline planning aid, not a tax return or filing confirmation.
Keep completed notes securely; never include passwords or one-time codes.

WORK THROUGH THIS EXAMPLE
Write to the contractor identifying the tax month, payment dates and statement required. Keep the request and response. If duplicates cannot be obtained, use HMRC’s published missing-statement route rather than inventing deductions by reversing the bank payment.

ADDITIONAL CHECK
 HMRC’s current route is to write to PT Operations, HM Revenue and Customs, BX9 1BX. Include your name, address and UTR; the contractor’s name and address; its tax reference if known; payment dates or tax months; and why statements or duplicates are unavailable.  Check the  official missing-statement instructions  before posting, and retain a copy. Requesting evidence does not itself confirm the deduction amount or extend a return deadline.
