ADDITIONAL CHECK
   Current or previous-year income:  eligible losses can be considered against the relevant income, subject to restrictions.   Future profits of the same trade:  unused qualifying losses may be carried forward.   Early trade losses:  the first four trading years can involve special carry-back rules.   Terminal losses:  a qualifying loss in the final 12 months can be set against relevant trade profits, with a specific carry-back order.   Use  HMRC’s 2025/26 loss helpsheet  for the applicable claims, deadlines and restrictions. General-income claims can affect allowances; partial claims are not freely selectable. Record the claim and unused balance. Overseas trades with a FIG claim need separate checking.
