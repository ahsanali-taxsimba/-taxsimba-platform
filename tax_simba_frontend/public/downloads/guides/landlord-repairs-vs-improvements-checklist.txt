WORK THROUGH THIS EXAMPLE
A contractor invoice covers repainting, replacing a broken fitting and adding an extension. Ask for the work to be itemised, with before-and-after descriptions. A single payment can contain different kinds of expenditure that should not be forced into one expense label.

DECISION TO RESOLVE
Can the cost of each distinct repair, replacement or improvement be evidenced?
