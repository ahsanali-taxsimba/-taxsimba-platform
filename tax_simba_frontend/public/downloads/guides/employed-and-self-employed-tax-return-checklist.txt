This is an optional offline planning aid, not a tax return or a filing confirmation.
Keep completed notes securely. Do not add passwords or one-time security codes.

PRACTICAL TASK
Reconcile the side business without counting your wages twice Suppose £500 moves from your salary account to your business account to pay a supplier. That transfer is not another £500 customer sale. Keep the transfer identifiable and give the accountant the supplier invoice separately. Make a two-part cover sheet: employment documents and tax deducted on one side; business income, costs and accounting period on the other. Add any other income below it. This helps the accountant distinguish the source of funds from the income being taxed.
