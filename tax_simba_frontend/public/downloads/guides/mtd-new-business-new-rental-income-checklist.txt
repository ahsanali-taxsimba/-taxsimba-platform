ADDITIONAL CHECK
 HMRC says an existing MTD user can choose to create digital records for a new source earlier, but does not have to do so until after submitting the first MTD tax return containing that source’s income or expenses. Keep the underlying ordinary tax records in the meantime.  For example, HMRC describes a business started in May 2027 whose first return includes 2027/28 income, due by 31 January 2029; compulsory digital records for that new business start from 6 April 2029. Confirm the timing for your own source rather than assuming every new business needs immediate quarterly updates.
