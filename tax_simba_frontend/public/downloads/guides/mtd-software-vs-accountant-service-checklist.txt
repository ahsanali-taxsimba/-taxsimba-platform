PRACTICAL TASK
Test the service with one real month Before choosing, describe a typical month: cash sales, card settlements, expenses, missing receipts and any property income. Ask who handles each item and what you must approve. A demo showing a connected bank account does not explain who reconciles the books or resolves a doubtful expense. Ask TaxSimba for the responsibilities confirmed in your engagement. In particular, check whether bookkeeping is done for you or whether you supply records ready for review. Keep the answer beside the price so the package matches the amount of work you can realistically do.
