WORK THROUGH THIS EXAMPLE
The lender requests two tax years, but the latest return was amended. Obtain the relevant tax calculations and overviews, checking the years and updated figures. A draft prepared before amendment can conflict with the HMRC record and delay the application.

DECISION TO RESOLVE
Do all requested documents relate to the correct years and the final filed position?
