PRACTICAL TASK
Keep two document requests separate If the accountant asks for your 2025/26 P60 while also checking 2026/27 business records, both requests can be correct. Reply with the tax year in each filename and message. For example, label an employment document “P60 2025-26” and the current trade export “Trade records 6 April to 5 October 2026”. Before approving anything, ask: which year does this submission finish? Keep a small tracker with one line for the earlier return and another for each current MTD business. A completed item in one line does not close the others.
