This is an optional offline planning aid, not a tax return or a filing confirmation.
Keep completed notes securely. Do not add passwords or one-time security codes.

PRACTICAL TASK
A useful order for your first checks Read the notice and check the signup in your HMRC account. List the continuing businesses and records already held. Check software and agent arrangements, including who can file. Identify outstanding updates and agree the catch-up work. Keep the earlier Self Assessment return on its own task list. Bring those five answers to an enquiry. They help distinguish a straightforward setup from missing-records work. If the notice looks wrong, use the guide to disputing MTD signup rather than buying a service on the assumption that the notice must be correct.
