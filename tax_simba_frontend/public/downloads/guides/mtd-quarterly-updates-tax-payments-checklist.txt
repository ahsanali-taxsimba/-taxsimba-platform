This is an optional offline planning aid, not a tax return or a filing confirmation.
Keep completed notes securely. Do not add passwords or one-time security codes.

PRACTICAL TASK
Label the payment before you make it Suppose your accountant charges £40 a month and the software shows an estimated £3,600 annual tax figure. Twelve service payments of £40 total £480 paid to the provider. They do not reduce the £3,600 estimate or count as tax paid to HMRC. Keep a simple payment list: payee, reference, amount, due date and confirmation. Use HMRC’s actual statement for tax amounts due. Do not divide the estimate by four and assume the result is a quarterly bill.
