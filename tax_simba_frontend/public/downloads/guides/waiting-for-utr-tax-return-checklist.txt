PRACTICAL TASK
If the reference still cannot be found For a new application, enter the registration details in HMRC’s reply-time checker . If the expected date has passed, or an existing UTR cannot be retrieved, use Self Assessment enquiries . Keep the enquiry date, acknowledgement and any response with your filing notice. Tell the accountant what remains unresolved. Use your individual Self Assessment UTR. A limited company’s Corporation Tax UTR is a different reference. Resolve the reference problem while preparing the figures rather than delaying both jobs.
