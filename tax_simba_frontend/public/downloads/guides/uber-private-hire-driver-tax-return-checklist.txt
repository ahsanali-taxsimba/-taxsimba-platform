PRACTICAL TASK
Before comparing mileage with actual vehicle costs Identify the vehicle type and previous claims first. HMRC excludes certain commercially designed vehicles, including black cabs, from simplified mileage. Prior capital-allowance or purchase-expense claims can also prevent its use. A private-hire car is not automatically treated the same as every taxi. Keep a dated business-mile log alongside actual running costs so an eligible comparison can be made. The tax year matters: HMRC lists 45p for the first 10,000 qualifying car or goods-vehicle miles before 6 April 2026, rising to 55p for 2026/27. The rate above 10,000 remains 25p. Check the linked mileage guide before using a rate.
