PRACTICAL TASK
Use HMRC’s appeal route for the actual penalty HMRC’s Self Assessment appeal checker directs you to the applicable online route or forms SA370 and SA371. Follow the notice and keep proof of sending the appeal. Record the penalty issue date immediately. HMRC normally allows 30 days from issue to contact it or appeal; explain any late appeal separately. Track the appeal, the return and the unpaid tax as separate tasks so sending a letter does not leave the original problem unresolved.
