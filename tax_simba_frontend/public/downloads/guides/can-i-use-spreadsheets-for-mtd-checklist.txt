TaxSimba — Can I Use Spreadsheets for Making Tax Digital?
Preparation worksheet

Optional offline planning aid, not a tax return or filing confirmation.
Keep completed notes securely; never include passwords or one-time codes.

WORK THROUGH THIS EXAMPLE
Before a deadline, ask how your workbook is imported into the compatible filing system and how errors are corrected. Test a small sample and compare the resulting totals. Sending a spreadsheet by email is not proof that the filing software has processed it or HMRC has received an update.

DECISION TO RESOLVE
Can you follow one record through the agreed digital process without rekeying reported data?
