WORK THROUGH THIS EXAMPLE
You discover that your side business may need reporting. First check the filing requirement, then confirm registration, gather the correct year’s figures, review the draft, obtain filing evidence and arrange any payment. Put these tasks on a timeline so registration is not mistaken for a finished return.
