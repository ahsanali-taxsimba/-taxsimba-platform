PRACTICAL TASK
Add expenses to the example Suppose earlier cumulative income was £12,000 and expenses were £3,000. New-period income is £15,000 and expenses are £4,000. Before corrections, the next totals are £27,000 income and £7,000 expenses. If £240 of the earlier expenses was private and must be removed, cumulative expenses become £6,760. The calculator below lets you change those numbers. Enter a correction only once. If the starting cumulative expenses already exclude the £240, subtracting it again would create a second mistake.
