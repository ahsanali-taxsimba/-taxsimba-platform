WORK THROUGH THIS EXAMPLE
Choose one supplier invoice. Find the digital entry, check its date, amount and category, then find the supporting document again. If the entry is duplicated or cannot be traced, fix the record rather than adding another copy of the image.

DECISION TO RESOLVE
