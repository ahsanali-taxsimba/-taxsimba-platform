WORK THROUGH THIS EXAMPLE
An agent receives £1,200 rent, keeps £120 in fees and pays £1,080 to you. The statement explains gross income of £1,200 and a separate £120 fee. Recording £1,080 rent and deducting £120 again counts the charge twice.

DECISION TO RESOLVE
Does the gross rent less each identified deduction equal the payout?
