ADDITIONAL CHECK
 Where the close-company director questions apply, the 2025/26 employment pages ask for the company’s name, registration number, dividends received from it and percentage shareholding. HMRC’s notes say to use the highest percentage shareholding in the year, calculated by reference to the nominal value of the shares.  Ask for the shareholding history as well as dividend vouchers. These employment-page details do not replace reporting the dividends in the relevant dividend section. Use the  current SA102 form and notes ; do not copy an older director questionnaire.
