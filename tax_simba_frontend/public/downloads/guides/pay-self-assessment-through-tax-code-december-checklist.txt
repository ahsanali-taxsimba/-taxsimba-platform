What the monthly deduction could look like An eligible £2,400 bill collected over 12 equal months would add £200 a month to PAYE deductions. This is an arithmetic example, not confirmation that HMRC will accept coding for your bill. Check three things after filing: the coding notice, the first affected payslip or pension statement, and any amount still due directly to HMRC. If the expected change is missing, query it before relying on that collection method. Keep payments on account in a separate line on your payment plan.
