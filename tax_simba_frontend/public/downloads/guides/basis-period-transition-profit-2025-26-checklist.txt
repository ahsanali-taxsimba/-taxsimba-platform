ADDITIONAL CHECK
 Illustration: £25,000 transition profit gives £5,000 in each of 2023/24, 2024/25, 2025/26 and 2026/27 under the ordinary 20% approach, leaving £5,000 for 2027/28. This assumes no acceleration election or earlier cessation.  An election to tax more earlier changes the remaining schedule. Permanent cessation before the fifth year brings the remaining balance into the cessation year under HMRC’s rule. Reconcile the original amount, annual allocations and balance before adding anything to current trading profit.
