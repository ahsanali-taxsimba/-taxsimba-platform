Preparation worksheet

Optional offline planning aid, not a tax return or filing confirmation.
Keep completed notes securely; never include passwords or one-time codes.

WORK THROUGH THIS EXAMPLE
A software subscription is active, but records are incomplete and no one has accepted the next filing. List signup, software authorisation, digital records, quarterly submissions and annual completion separately. That makes it easier to see whether the problem is access, bookkeeping or responsibility.

DECISION TO RESOLVE
Which part is ready, and which still has no named person responsible?
