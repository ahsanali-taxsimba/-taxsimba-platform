REVIEW CHECK
 HMRC lists a temporary 2026/27 exemption where the 2024/25 return included SA109 residence pages. If those pages were not included before but are reasonably expected for 2025/26 or 2026/27, an application may be needed. Relevant cases include non-residence, split-year treatment and certain FIG or overseas-workday-relief claims. Check the exact conditions and expiry; do not infer that foreign income alone creates an exemption.
