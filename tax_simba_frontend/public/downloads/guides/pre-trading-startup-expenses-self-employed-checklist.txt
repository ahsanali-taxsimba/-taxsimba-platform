WORK THROUGH THIS EXAMPLE
For each earlier cost, record the date, supplier, purpose, trade-start date and any private or capital element. Keep the evidence rather than labelling every purchase before the first sale as startup expenses. Training and equipment can need their own assessment.

DECISION TO RESOLVE
Would the cost qualify under the relevant conditions rather than merely fall within seven years?
