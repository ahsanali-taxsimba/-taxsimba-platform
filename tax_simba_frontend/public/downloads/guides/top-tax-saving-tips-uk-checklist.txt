WORK THROUGH THIS EXAMPLE
A £500 purchase is not a £500 tax saving. Before buying, ask whether the business needs it, whether relief is available and which year it affects. Review costs already incurred before creating new spending purely to obtain a deduction.

DECISION TO RESOLVE
Would the purchase still be worthwhile after paying its cost and allowing for any actual relief?
