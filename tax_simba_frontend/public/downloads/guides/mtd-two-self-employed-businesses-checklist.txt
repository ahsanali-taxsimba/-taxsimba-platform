WORK THROUGH THIS EXAMPLE
Two trades share a phone. Keep an explained allocation between them and any private use. Do not put the entire bill into both sets of records. Check first whether the activities are separate trades rather than inferring that from separate bank accounts.

DECISION TO RESOLVE
Can each trade’s figures reconcile without duplicating the shared expense?
